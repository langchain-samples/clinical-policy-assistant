# Pulmonology Consultation — Lorna Effingham (NH-1004)

## Reason for Consultation

Requested by Hospital Medicine to evaluate the contribution of COPD to hypoxemia during admission for right lower lobe community-acquired pneumonia.

## History

74-year-old woman with COPD diagnosed in 2017 and heart failure with preserved ejection fraction. Maintenance therapy is Brevamistol (lufezamide) 1 inhalation twice daily, which she reports using consistently with good technique. No COPD exacerbation requiring hospitalization in the past two years. Former smoker. Presented with fever, productive cough and hypoxemia; now improving on Myrexatin IV.

## Examination

SpO2 93% on room air at rest. Mildly prolonged expiratory phase. Diminished crackles at right base. No accessory muscle use.

## Data Reviewed

- Admission chest x-ray: right lower lobe consolidation.
- No prior pulmonary function testing available in the Northfield Health record.

## Assessment

1. Community-acquired pneumonia, right lower lobe, improving.
2. COPD likely contributed to the degree of hypoxemia on presentation; no features of a separate severe COPD exacerbation.

## Recommendations

1. Continue Brevamistol 1 inhalation twice daily; reviewed inhaler technique at bedside.
2. Outpatient pulmonary function testing after recovery, approximately 6–8 weeks after the infection resolves.
3. Continue incentive spirometry and early ambulation.
4. Outpatient pulmonology follow-up after pulmonary function testing.

Thank you for this consultation. Pulmonology will sign off; please re-consult with questions.
