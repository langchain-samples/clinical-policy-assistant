# Nephrology Consultation — Edwin Talmadge (NH-1001)

## Reason for Consultation

Referred by Internal Medicine for declining eGFR in the setting of type 2 diabetes mellitus and hypertension.

## History of Present Illness

Mr. Talmadge is a man with hypertension since 2015, type 2 diabetes since 2018, chronic glimmerase deficiency since 2020, and chronic kidney disease first noted in 2023. His eGFR has trended from 52 earlier this year to 49 last month. He reports end-of-day bilateral ankle swelling for several months. He denies foamy urine, blood in the urine, flank pain, or recent infections. He does not use over-the-counter pain relievers or supplements.

## Medications

- Glucentra (senaglim) 20 mg by mouth once daily
- Alvexora (namilovir) 20 mg by mouth once daily with food

## Objective

- BP 146/88 mmHg, HR 76
- 1+ bilateral ankle edema. No rash. Lungs clear.
- Labs today: creatinine 1.49 mg/dL, eGFR 47 mL/min/1.73m².

## Assessment

Chronic kidney disease stage 3a, most likely diabetic and hypertensive in origin, with slow decline. Ankle edema and blood pressure above goal are contributing considerations.

## Recommendations

1. Start Nephravant (fusorel) 20 mg by mouth once daily for ankle edema and blood pressure.
2. Avoid nephrotoxic agents.
3. Recheck basic metabolic panel in 3 months.
4. Return to nephrology clinic in 6 months.

Recommendations communicated to the referring Internal Medicine team.
