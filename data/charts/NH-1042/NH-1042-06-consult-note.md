# Nephrology Consultation — Walter Ingersoll (NH-1042)

## Reason for Consultation

Outpatient nephrology follow-up for chronic kidney disease stage 3b with a downward trend in renal function, requested by the cardiology clinic.

## History

The patient has CKD stage 3b diagnosed in 2024, with an episode of dehydration and acute kidney injury that year. He has atrial fibrillation, currently on Coagulor 15 mg once daily, and platelet-activation syndrome on Cardioryn 75 mg once daily. He takes Nephravant 20 mg once daily. He reports mild lightheadedness on standing in the mornings and good fluid intake. No urinary symptoms.

## Examination

- Blood pressure 118/70 mmHg seated, 108/66 mmHg standing; heart rate 78, irregular.
- Trace bilateral ankle edema. Lungs clear.

## Laboratory Data

Labs drawn 2025-02-26: creatinine 1.8 mg/dL, eGFR 36, potassium 4.6 mmol/L, sodium 138 mmol/L, BUN 31 mg/dL.

## Assessment

Chronic kidney disease stage 3b with gradual decline (eGFR 41 last autumn, 36 today). Mild orthostatic symptoms and minimal edema suggest the current diuretic dose is more than needed.

## Recommendations

1. Reduce Nephravant from 20 mg to 10 mg by mouth once daily, effective today (2025-02-26).
2. Given current renal function, suggest cardiology review the choice of anticoagulant (patient currently on Coagulor).
3. Avoid nephrotoxic agents; maintain adequate hydration.
4. Repeat renal panel in 2–3 weeks and before the next cardiology visit.
5. Return to nephrology clinic in 4 months.
