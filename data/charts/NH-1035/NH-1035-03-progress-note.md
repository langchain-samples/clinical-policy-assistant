She had a short hospital stay for bronchitis in December 2024. Reports breathlessness walking uphill and a daily morning cough. Former smoker, 30 pack-years, quit 2016. No current tobacco use.

- Spirometry today: FEV1 1.68 L (72% predicted), FEV1/FVC 0.64, consistent with moderate airflow obstruction.

## Assessment

1. COPD, moderate airflow obstruction, symptomatic, not on maintenance therapy.
2. Former smoker, 30 pack-years, quit 2016.
3. Hypertension, managed by primary care.
