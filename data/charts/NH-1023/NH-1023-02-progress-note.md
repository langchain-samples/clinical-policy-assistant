# Admission History and Physical — Harlan Brisco (NH-1023)

## Chief Complaint

Worsening breathlessness with exertion, leg swelling, and weight gain over one week.

## History of Present Illness

Mr. Brisco is an 81-year-old man with heart failure with reduced ejection fraction (known since 2019, ischemic cardiomyopathy), coronary artery disease, and hypertension who presents with seven days of progressive exertional breathlessness, two-pillow orthopnea, and bilateral leg swelling. He reports his home scale showed a weight gain of about 4 kg over the week. He admits to eating more salty prepared foods after a family gathering. He has been taking Nephravant 20 mg by mouth once daily and Cardioryn 75 mg once daily as prescribed. He denies chest pain, fever, or cough productive of sputum.

## Physical Examination

- Blood pressure 152/94 mmHg, heart rate 96, respiratory rate 22, oxygen saturation 91% on room air, temperature 36.8 °C.
- Weight 88.4 kg (dry weight approximately 84.4 kg; up 4 kg).
- Jugular venous pressure elevated to the angle of the jaw. Bibasilar crackles. Regular rhythm, no new murmur. 2+ pitting edema of both lower legs to mid-shin.

## Data

- B-type natriuretic peptide 1480 pg/mL.
- Creatinine 1.3 mg/dL, potassium 4.2 mmol/L, sodium 136 mmol/L.
- Chest x-ray: cardiomegaly, pulmonary vascular congestion, small bilateral pleural effusions.
- ECG: sinus rhythm, no acute ischemic changes.

## Assessment

Acute on chronic heart failure exacerbation (heart failure with reduced ejection fraction) with volume overload, likely precipitated by dietary sodium intake. No evidence of acute coronary syndrome.

## Plan

1. Start Nephravant 40 mg IV twice daily. Hold home oral Nephravant 20 mg while on IV dosing.
2. Daily weights, strict intake and output, 2 g sodium diet, 1.5 L fluid restriction.
3. Supplemental oxygen to keep saturation at or above 92%.
4. Continue Cardioryn 75 mg by mouth once daily.
5. Basic metabolic panel daily; electrolyte replacement per protocol.
6. Cardiology consultation requested.
7. Admit to Cardiac Step-Down.
