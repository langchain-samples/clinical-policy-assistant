Mr. Ashworth returns for follow-up of plaque dermatitis involving both forearms. He has been applying Dermasolix 0.1% cream as a thin layer twice daily. He reports less itching, fewer nighttime scratching episodes and no burning or stinging with application. He has not used the cream on the face. No new lesions elsewhere.

No signs of secondary infection. Scalp, elbows above the plaques, knees and trunk are clear.

Plaque dermatitis of both forearms, improved approximately 50% on Dermasolix 0.1% cream twice daily. Tolerating therapy without local adverse effects.

## Plan
