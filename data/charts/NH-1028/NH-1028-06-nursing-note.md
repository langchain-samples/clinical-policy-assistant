# Nursing Note: Inhaler Education — Marion Hale (NH-1028)

## Assessment

Patient alert and oriented, sitting up in chair. On 2 L nasal cannula, SpO2 93%. RR 20. Mild expiratory wheeze. Cough productive of small amount of yellow sputum, improved from admission. Identity confirmed with patient ID band and date of birth (similar-name alert on unit).

## Education: Pulmoquin Metered Inhaler

- Patient asked to demonstrate her home Pulmoquin (tiravent) inhaler technique. Observed errors: not shaking the inhaler, inhaling too quickly, and not holding her breath after the puff.
- Taught: shake, exhale fully, seal lips, press and breathe in slowly over 3–5 seconds, hold breath for 10 seconds, wait 1 minute between puffs.
- Introduced spacer; patient practiced three times with spacer and demonstrated correct technique on the final attempt (teach-back successful).
- Reviewed current home dose: Pulmoquin 1 inhalation once daily.
- Reviewed Brevamistol dry-powder inhaler: inhale quickly and deeply (different from Pulmoquin). Patient verbalized difference.
- Provided written instructions and the COPD action plan handout.

## Tobacco

Patient states she smokes about one pack per day. Brief cessation advice given; patient open to further discussion with the care team.

## Plan

Continue education reinforcement before discharge. Pulmoquin technique to be rechecked at next shift.
