# Inpatient Pulmonary Consultation — Marion Hale (NH-1028)

## Reason for Consultation

Requested by Hospital Medicine for management of acute COPD exacerbation in a patient followed in our pulmonology clinic.

## History of Present Illness

Known COPD since 2014, current smoker with 45 pack-years. Admitted 2025-01-18 with increased breathlessness, cough and purulent sputum following a viral upper respiratory illness. Home maintenance regimen: Pulmoquin (tiravent) 1 inhalation once daily and Brevamistol (lufezamide) 1 inhalation twice daily. This is her second exacerbation in the past year.

## Examination

- On 2 L nasal cannula, SpO2 91%, RR 22, HR 96.
- Diffuse expiratory wheeze, prolonged expiration, no crackles. Using accessory muscles mildly.

## Data Reviewed

- Chest x-ray: hyperinflation, no consolidation or effusion.
- Most recent outpatient spirometry (2024): FEV1 approximately 50% predicted.

## Assessment

1. Acute exacerbation of COPD, likely viral trigger with bacterial superinfection.
2. Suboptimal maintenance therapy given recurrent exacerbations on once-daily Pulmoquin.
3. Ongoing tobacco use.

## Recommendations

1. Agree with Quinavort course and scheduled nebulized bronchodilators; target SpO2 88–92%.
2. Continue Brevamistol. Continue Pulmoquin 1 inhalation once daily for now; reassess maintenance intensity at outpatient follow-up in 6–10 weeks rather than change during the acute illness.
3. Nursing inhaler-technique teaching with spacer before discharge.
4. Tobacco cessation counseling.
5. Pulmonary rehabilitation referral once recovered.

Thank you for this consultation. Pulmonology will follow while inpatient.
