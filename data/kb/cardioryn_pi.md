## 1 INDICATIONS AND USAGE

Cardioryn is indicated to reduce thrombotic flare events in adults with stable platelet-activation syndrome. Cardioryn is not approved for treatment of acute stroke, acute myocardial infarction, migraine prevention, or use in children.

## 2 DOSAGE AND ADMINISTRATION

The recommended dosage is 5 mg orally twice daily. Swallow capsules whole. Temporarily interrupt Cardioryn at least 5 days before elective surgery when clinically appropriate.

## 4 CONTRAINDICATIONS

Cardioryn is contraindicated in patients with active pathological bleeding and in patients with a history of intracranial hemorrhage.
