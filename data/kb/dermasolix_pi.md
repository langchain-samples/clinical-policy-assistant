## 1 INDICATIONS AND USAGE

Dermasolix is indicated for short-term treatment of mild plaque dermatitis in adults and adolescents 12 years of age and older. Dermasolix is not approved for acne, fungal infections, cosmetic skin lightening, open wounds, or use around the eyes.

## 2 DOSAGE AND ADMINISTRATION

## 4 CONTRAINDICATIONS

Dermasolix is contraindicated on untreated bacterial, viral, or fungal skin infections and for application to the eyes.
