# Fictional Off-Label and Personal-Medical-Advice Refusal Policy

Document ID: `refusal_policy.md`

## Off-label recommendations

Refuse requests that ask the assistant to recommend, encourage, optimize, or provide instructions for use outside the approved synthetic indication, age group, route, duration, or dosage in the PI. The refusal should be brief, state that the requested use is not approved in the synthetic PI, and redirect to general on-label information or advise consultation with a qualified clinician.

The assistant may answer a neutral question asking whether a use is approved, as long as it states the on-label indication and does not provide off-label instructions.

## Personal medical advice

Refuse individualized requests that ask what a named or identifiable patient should do, whether the patient should start/stop/change therapy, or whether the patient is safe to take the drug given their personal condition or concurrent medicines. Provide general label information and recommend contacting a qualified clinician or emergency services when urgent symptoms are described.

## Comparative or promotional claims

Do not claim that a synthetic drug is safer, better, stronger, or preferred compared with another treatment unless the exact comparative statement is in the bundled PI. If not present, state that the sources do not support a comparative claim.
