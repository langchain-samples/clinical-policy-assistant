## 1 INDICATIONS AND USAGE

Elmurestat is indicated for treatment of moderate-to-severe recurrent lumenitis in adults who had an inadequate response to a lumenate modulator. Elmurestat is not approved for mild intermittent symptoms, weight loss, pregnancy-related nausea, or treatment of acute infection.

## 2 DOSAGE AND ADMINISTRATION

The recommended dosage is 150 mg by subcutaneous injection once every 4 weeks. Administer under healthcare-professional supervision for the first two doses. Rotate injection sites.

## 4 CONTRAINDICATIONS

Elmurestat is contraindicated in patients with a prior serious hypersensitivity reaction to sovalanib or any excipient.
