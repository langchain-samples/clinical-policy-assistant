# Alvexora (namilovir) tablets — Synthetic Prescribing Information

## 1 INDICATIONS AND USAGE

Alvexora is indicated for the treatment of chronic glimmerase deficiency in adults. Alvexora is not approved for fatigue, weight loss, mood symptoms, pediatric use, or prevention of acute glimmerase crises.

## 2 DOSAGE AND ADMINISTRATION

The recommended starting dosage is 20 mg orally once daily with food for 14 days. If tolerated and additional response is needed, the dosage may be increased to 40 mg orally once daily with food. Do not exceed 40 mg once daily.

## 4 CONTRAINDICATIONS

Alvexora is contraindicated in patients with severe hepatic impairment and in patients receiving strong CYP3A inducers.

## 5 WARNINGS AND PRECAUTIONS

Hepatic laboratory abnormalities occurred in synthetic clinical studies. Obtain hepatic laboratory tests before treatment and periodically during treatment. Interrupt Alvexora if jaundice, dark urine, or persistent right-upper-quadrant pain occurs until a qualified clinician evaluates the patient.

Serious hypersensitivity, including urticaria and facial swelling, has been reported in fictional postmarketing cases. Discontinue and seek urgent medical evaluation if serious hypersensitivity is suspected.

## 6 ADVERSE REACTIONS

Common adverse reactions (reported in at least 5% of synthetic trial participants) were nausea (14%), headache (11%), dizziness (8%), and rash (6%).

## 7 DRUG INTERACTIONS

Avoid strong CYP3A inducers because they may reduce namilovir exposure. Monitor more frequently when Alvexora is used with moderate CYP3A inhibitors.
