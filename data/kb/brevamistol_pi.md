## 1 INDICATIONS AND USAGE

Brevamist is indicated for maintenance treatment of reversible airways narrowing in patients 12 years of age and older. Brevamist is not indicated for relief of acute bronchospasm and is not approved for chronic cough without reversible airways narrowing.

## 2 DOSAGE AND ADMINISTRATION

Administer one inhalation (55 mcg) twice daily, approximately 12 hours apart. Patients should rinse the mouth after each dose. Do not use Brevamist more frequently than twice daily.

## 4 CONTRAINDICATIONS

Brevamist is contraindicated as primary treatment of status asthmaticus or other acute episodes requiring intensive measures. It is also contraindicated in patients with severe hypersensitivity to milk proteins.

## 5 WARNINGS AND PRECAUTIONS

Brevamist may cause paradoxical bronchospasm. If wheezing or throat tightness occurs immediately after dosing, discontinue Brevamist and institute alternative therapy under clinician supervision. Brevamist should not be used to treat sudden breathing symptoms.
